# Changelog — Fidelia Installateur (team and field-agent site)

What the installer site does, newest first. It has no version numbers: the
test site deploys `integration`. Entries are grouped by the day the work
landed. It was **Djassa Installateur** until 6 October 2026, then **Hossouko
Installateur**, and is **Fidelia Installateur** since 9 October.

## 2026-10-09

- **Suppressions**: account deletion requests from merchants, cashiers and
  field agents; delete the account after a confirmation, or refuse with a note.
- **Renamed to Fidelia**, with the new icon; browsers and home-screen
  shortcuts drop the old cached icons.
- Deploys from `integration`.

## 2026-10-06

- **Restaurant** category in the field-agent tools.
- Renamed to Hossouko.

## 2026-10-04

First version, for the Fidelia team and field agents, signed in by phone
number and SMS code.

- **Admins**: shops and their photos and videos, merchant sign-up requests
  with the approval checklist, account recoveries, users and roles, errors
  reported by the apps.
- **Field agents**: enrol a shop on site, and see the shops they enrolled.
- Installable on the phone's home screen.
